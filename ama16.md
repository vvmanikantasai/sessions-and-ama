# AMA

## Q1. What is prompt engineering?

**Answer:**  
Prompt engineering is the process of writing clear and effective instructions (prompts) to get the best possible response from an AI model.

---

## Q2. What is meant by calibration in an AI model?

**Answer:**  
Calibration is how well an AI's confidence matches its actual accuracy. A well-calibrated model is confident only when it is likely to be correct.

---

## Q3. What is extended thinking in an AI model?

**Answer:**  
Extended thinking is when an AI spends more time reasoning through a complex problem before generating the final answer, helping improve accuracy on difficult tasks.

---

## Q4. What is temperature?

**Answer:**  
Temperature controls how random or creative the AI's responses are. Lower values produce more predictable answers, while higher values produce more diverse and creative ones.

---

## Q5. What is the difference between Top-k and Top-p?

**Answer:**  
**Top-k** selects the next word from a fixed number of the most likely options. **Top-p** selects from the smallest group of words whose combined probability reaches a chosen threshold, making it more flexible.

---

## Q6. Tell me about some prompt engineering techniques.

**Answer:**  
Some common techniques are **Zero-shot Prompting**, **Few-shot Prompting**, **Chain-of-Thought Prompting**, **Role Prompting**, and **Self-Consistency**, which help improve the quality and accuracy of AI responses.
